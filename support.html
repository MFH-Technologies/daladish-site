Support

FAQs

Is DalaDish free?

Yes. No paywall and nothing to buy in the app.

How do I sign in?

With your email address and password. New to DalaDish? Tap Create an account, and we email you a code once to confirm the address. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password and we email you a code so you can choose a new one. Signing in with Apple or Google is coming.

The email with my code hasn't arrived

Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from DalaDish. Then tap Send a new code and use the code in the newest email.

How do we plan together as a family?

In Me, tap Make a family code and give it to the other adult. They tap I have a family code, create their own account and type the code, and you tap Let them in. You then share one plan and one shopping list.

Where do the prices come from?

From the supermarket's public website, with the store and the date we read them. Prices change, so the till price applies. DalaDish is independent and not linked to any supermarket.

Allergies, halaal, kosher and no pork

Choose from 12 common allergens and any foods you don't eat, and pick halaal, kosher or no pork under Diet. DalaDish leaves out what doesn't fit. Because this is health or religious information, we ask your consent before keeping it. We don't certify food, so always check the label.

Diabetes, high blood pressure and pregnancy

Under Health needs in setup, or later in Me, choose less sugar and steady carbs, less salt, less saturated fat, or expecting a baby. Every meal then keeps to a limit per adult plate, often with a smaller starch portion and veg on the side, and every recipe shows its carbs, sugar, fibre and salt. It's a guide, not medical advice: your doctor or dietitian knows best.

What happens to my information?

We use it to plan your meals and your list, and it's stored in the European Union. If you say yes, we also count how you use the app, with a random code instead of your name, and include your household in anonymous trend reports that we may sell. Both are off unless you switch them on. You can change what we keep in Me, then Your privacy choices. The detail is in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap Me, then Delete my account. Or follow the steps on Delete your DalaDish account.
